Beta Testing

Until TournaQ Volley reaches the stores, each platform hands out the current build through its own test programme. Same app, a version or two ahead — it just takes two steps to install rather than one. This route closes once the official releases land above.

Android

Google Play hands the test build to a named list of testers only. Join the group first, and the store page will then let you install.

  1. 1 Join the tester group

    The group is tournaq-testing@googlegroups.com. Google Play checks the account your phone is signed in with against it, so join with that same Google account.

    Join on Google Groups
  2. 2 Install from Google Play

    Once you are in the group, the Play Store page offers the test build. If it still says the app is unavailable, give it a few minutes.

    Open Google Play

iOS

Apple ships test builds through TestFlight. Install TestFlight once, and the invite link below opens straight into it.

  1. 1 Install TestFlight

    Apple's own app for test builds. Free, from the App Store, and you only do this once.

    Get TestFlight
  2. 2 Install TournaQ Volley

    Open the invite and TestFlight takes over — accept, install, and the app is on your home screen.

    Open in TestFlight
