Tournament Hub

Where a tournament is built. Pick the mode, bring the people in, say how much time and how many courts you have — TournaQ works out the rest.

Setting one up
1
Pick the modeLeague, elimination, Classic, Swiss, or one of the scrambles.
2
Bring in the playersPull them from Administration — filter by group, search, or add the whole group at once — or type in new ones on the spot.
3
Courts and formatHow many courts, how big the teams, how long a game lasts.
4
Rounds and timingTournaQ builds the schedule and puts every game into a slot.
5
StartThe tournament opens and the first games are ready to score.
Run the same thing again
Copy a finished tournament and you get the same participants and the same settings, ready for next week — one tap instead of the whole setup.
