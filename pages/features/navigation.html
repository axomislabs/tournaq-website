App Navigation

The home screen splits the app into three: TournaQ Arena for every game and tournament, Administration for players, teams and groups, and More for everything alongside them.

Every route follows the same shape — a top-level destination, then a hub, then the detail, and back the same way. No hidden paths and no dead ends, which matters when you are holding a phone in one hand at the side of a court.

Best for

  • Getting oriented before running your first session
  • Finding a screen you have seen once and cannot place
  • Knowing what lives where before you need it mid-match

Opening the app

Two screens between launching TournaQ and choosing what to run.

Home

TournaQ Arena, Administration and More. Three cards, and everything in the app sits under one of them.

The whole screen

Below the three destinations sit the shortcuts to whatever you were last doing, so a session in progress is one tap from launch.

The Arena

Every format the app can run, grouped by the kind of session it suits — and everything you have already run, underneath.

Nine formats, three groups

Quick Games at the top, then Single Competitions & Socials where you enter as a player, then Team Competitions. The number on a card is how many of those you have run.

History underneath

The same screen carries your tournament history, grouped by mode with a count against each, so a previous event is a couple of taps away.

Filtered history

Pick a mode and the history narrows to it — the fastest route back into something you ran last month.

Finding an old event

Nothing is deleted when a session ends. Every tournament stays on the device with all its results.

All tournaments

The full list across every mode, with the date, the player or team count and how far each got. Completed and in-progress events sit together, marked.

Administration

The other top-level destination: players, teams and groups saved once and reused in every format. It has its own page here.

Everything else

The screens that sit beside the main flow — settings, contact, and the things you look at once.

More

Contact & About, Sponsoring & Promo, and Settings. The screens you need occasionally, kept out of the way of the ones you need mid-match.

Settings

Four language options — Automatic, English, Deutsch, Español — and a Privacy Options link for managing ad consent.

Contact & About

Social links, support channels, the legal documents and the app version, in one place.

Sponsoring & Promo

Advertising and partnership options, for clubs and events that want to put their name on a session.

Become a tester

The route into the beta, for people who want the next formats before they ship.

Coming soon

Where a format that is not built yet takes you, so the Arena never has a card that does nothing.

Every navigation feature

  • Available
    Three top-level destinations

    The Home screen splits the app into TournaQ Arena for all games and tournaments, Administration for players, teams, and groups, and More for everything alongside them. Everything else branches off these three.

  • Available
    More

    More collects the screens that sit beside the main flow: Contact & About (social links, support channels, legal documents, and the app version), Sponsoring & Promo (advertising and partnership opportunities), and Settings.

  • Available
    Straightforward navigation

    Every route follows the same shape — from a top-level destination into a hub, from the hub down to the next level of detail, and back the same way. No hidden paths and no dead ends.

  • Available
    TournaQ Arena mode hub

    Every game and tournament mode is grouped into three categories — Quick Games, Single Competitions & Socials, and Team Competitions — each a large tappable card.

  • Available
    Mode availability at a glance

    Available modes are shown in gold with a badge counting the tournaments already played in that mode. Modes still in development appear in grey with a "Soon" badge.

  • Available
    Built-in mode help

    An info icon on every mode card explains the format before you commit to it, with a "Learn more" link through to the website.

  • Available
    Three-step path to a scorecard

    Pick a mode, configure the setup, open a match. The path is identical in every format — learn it once and it applies to all of them.

  • Available
    Tournament dashboard

    Each tournament opens on a dashboard showing the full schedule grouped by round, with team names, courts, times, match status, and a completion percentage. Tap any match to score it.

  • Available
    Tournament history by mode

    Past tournaments are saved on the device and listed in the Arena's history panel, grouped by mode with a total count — so any previous event is a couple of taps away.

  • Available
    Coming Soon feature cards

    Modes and features still in development appear as cards in the Arena and on the Home screen, each linking through to the website, the feedback form, and email.

  • Available
    Language & privacy settings

    Settings offers four language options — Automatic, English, Deutsch, Español — and a Privacy Options link for managing ad consent choices.
