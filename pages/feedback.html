Find your spot, then tell us

The map carries the same structure as the app. Pick the place you were when something went wrong, confused you, or worked so well it deserves saying — then write it down right there. Every spot shows what it looks like in the app, so you can see you picked the right one. On a phone the map sits behind the button above.

Notes stay on this device until you send them. Nothing is uploaded while you type, and you can collect as many as you like before handing them over in one go.
