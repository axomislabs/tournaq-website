Mode family

Queue Modes

Earn your spot, win strikes or escape — while challengers always rotate. Four modes share one engine, and three settings decide how it feels. Three of them are scramble competitions, where every result is yours alone; Royal Duo is a team competition, where your pair stays together all the way.

Setting 1 · Who assigns the challengers
Assignment
ManualAn admin assigns the challengers. Winners move onto the court automatically.
AutomatedThe system works out the queue. An admin orchestrates the game but stays off court.
Auto AllplayThe system works out the queue, and the admins rotate in and play too.
Setting 2 · What happens with an odd player — scramble competitions only
Odd player handling

Scramble competitions only: a team competition arrives in pairs, so there is never a short team to fill.

PlaceholderAny available player joins the short team.
JumperAvailable players rotate fairly through the round to fill the short team.
Setting 3 · What the table is sorted by
Standings
Ranking pointsEqualised points awarded by where you placed on your court each round. Score at least once and you earn at least one.
Strikes / escapesThe actual games won, round by round.
PointsThe actual points won while on court, round by round.

How many courts you can run
Queue modes honour the court count you set, down to a minimum number of players per court. If the roster cannot fill them, TournaQ blocks the court rather than running a broken queue.
